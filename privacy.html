Privacy Policy

Prove It  ·  Last updated: 12 June 2026

Prove It is an iOS app that helps you reduce screen time by blocking selected apps until you complete a photo challenge. This policy explains what we collect, what we never collect, how we store things, and the services we use.

Short version: Your blocked-app choices, Screen Time data, challenge photos, and unlock history stay on your device. We use a small number of third-party services for subscriptions, analytics, diagnostics, and attribution.

1. Introduction

We built Prove It around a simple idea: the sensitive data created inside the app should stay yours. The sections below explain how that works in practice.

2. Information We Collect

Information you provide directly

During setup, you may provide your first name, age range, phone-use estimates, daily goal, discovery source, and commitment signature. This information is stored on your device. Limited setup answers, such as age range, goals, discovery source, and onboarding completion, may also be used for product analytics.

App data stored on your device

Your blocked apps, categories, websites, unlock history, challenge photos, and Screen Time configuration are stored locally. Challenge photos are processed on-device and are not uploaded.

Information collected automatically

To keep the app stable and useful, we collect limited technical and usage data: app version, build number, device and operating-system information, app events, permission results, purchase or restore status, and aggregate block or unlock settings. We do not send the names of apps or websites you block.

Permissions

Prove It may ask for Screen Time, camera, notifications, and, where needed, App Tracking Transparency permission. Each permission is used only for the feature that needs it.

3. Information We Do NOT Collect

We want to be explicit about this. Prove It does not collect or transmit:

  • Challenge photos or unlock history photos
  • Photos from your camera roll
  • The names of apps, categories, or websites you block
  • Screen Time reports
  • Precise location or GPS data
  • Contacts, calendar, health data, clipboard contents, or browsing history

We do not sell your personal information.

4. How We Use Your Information

We use the information described above to:

  • Run blocking, unlock challenges, reminders, and subscriptions
  • Personalise the app experience
  • Fix bugs and improve reliability
  • Understand which features are useful
  • Measure onboarding, purchases, and marketing performance

5. Data Storage and Security

Local app data is stored in Prove It's private container on your device. We do not run a server that stores your blocked-app list, Screen Time data, challenge photos, or unlock history.

6. Third-Party Services

Prove It uses a small number of third-party services to operate. Each is listed below, with a clear note on what they receive.

Apple platform services

Prove It uses Apple services such as the App Store, Screen Time, Family Controls, notifications, and system permission prompts. Your use of these services is governed by Apple's privacy policy.

Purchases and subscriptions

  • RevenueCat for subscription management

Analytics and attribution

We use the following services to understand how Prove It performs, which features are used, and whether marketing campaigns are working:

  • Firebase Analytics for event counts, diagnostics, crash reporting, and performance tools
  • Mixpanel for feature-usage analytics
  • Microsoft Clarity for interaction and performance insights, with content masked by default
  • Meta SDK / Meta App Events for attribution and advertising measurement

These services do not receive your challenge photos, unlock history photos, blocked-app names, Screen Time reports, or camera-roll content from Prove It. They receive app events, aggregated metrics, diagnostics, or attribution events only.

7. Data Retention

Local data stays on your device until it is replaced by newer entries or deleted with the app. Subscription and analytics records are retained by the relevant third-party services under their own policies.

8. Your Rights

You can revoke app permissions in iOS Settings. You can also request access, correction, or deletion of data by emailing maxflameapps@gmail.com.

9. Children's Privacy

Prove It does not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted here with an updated date.
