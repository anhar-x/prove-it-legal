Frequently Asked Questions

How do I restore my subscription?

Open Prove It and use the in-app restore purchases option. If your subscription still does not appear, email support and we will help you troubleshoot it.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple. On your iPhone, go to Settings > Apple ID > Subscriptions, then select Prove It to manage or cancel the subscription.

Why is app blocking not working?

Prove It depends on Apple Screen Time / Family Controls permissions. If blocking stops working, check that permission is still enabled and that the block is still configured inside the app.

Does Prove It upload my challenge photos?

No. Challenge verification runs on-device. Photos used for verification are not uploaded to external servers.
